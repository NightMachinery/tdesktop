# Birthday reminders

Open **Settings → Purple → Birthday Reminders** on Desktop. The audience for
each account starts at **Nobody**. **My Contacts** includes eligible Telegram
contacts. **Everybody** also includes other people whose birthday Purple has
received from Telegram and can still see. The app uses the ordinary birthdays
request and its existing peer data. It does not scan chats or request dates
from accounts that have kept them private.

Click the chat-list banner to see the people with birthdays today. Select a
person to open Telegram's gift chooser or their chat. **Never Remind** excludes
that person from both the banner and the birthday-triggered gift button in chat.
Remove the person from **Never Remind** in the settings to resume reminders.
Closing the banner hides birthday reminders for the rest of the local day.
The banner and its recipient list respect the current Work Mode visibility.

The audience and exclusions are stored in the account's local Desktop
preferences. Each signed-in account has its own rules. Telegram's public
birthday privacy and manual gifting are independent of those rules.
